# Hit placement: isolated correction, not yet integrated

The frozen candidate25 renderer can move a bullet mark away from the actual incoming ray onto another folded body surface. `npc_postmortem_bullet_marks.gd:306` first correctly intersects the incoming projectile direction with this actor's original posed triangles. At line312 it then discards that triangle/barycentric center and casts again along the triangle normal. `_probe`, line252, chooses the largest projected z across a ±0.20 × model-unit interval. A nearby limb or fold ahead of the contacted surface can win the second query even when the incoming bullet ray never intersects that limb. Rim vertices use the same rule, so parts of the mark can attach to the neighbouring bone and move with it.

`geometry01` proves this using the actual frozen and proposed renderer scripts, a bent contact surface and an overlapping surface bound to another skeleton bone. It does not instantiate the game.
