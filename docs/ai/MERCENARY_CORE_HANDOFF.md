## Adapter contract

`createMercenarySquad({getMember,getTarget,moveMember,performEffect,now,onAction,scanReviveTargets,balance})` uses metre positions and epoch seconds (`Date.now()/1000` default). `getMember(id)` returns live `{id,position:{x,y,z},hp,maxHp,dead,downed,available,deathConfirmed,followArrived}`. `getTarget(id)` returns live `{id,kind,position,valid,hp,dead,downed,revivable,locked,opened,cut,cuttable,destroyed,bombable}`. Target kinds: npc/player/safe/door/fence/vehicle. The adapter controls suitability, collision/navigation, actual effects, payment and ownership.

`moveMember(id,target,{phase,stopDistance})` requests ordinary movement; null target means stop. Core never writes positions or HP. `onAction(id,{kind,phase,progress,targetId,startedAt,duration})` carries animation state. Phases: approach, working, retreat, countdown, completed, cancelled. Durations and timestamps are seconds.

`performEffect(payload)` MUST synchronously return `true` or `{ok:true}` only after authoritative success. False, `{ok:false}`, undefined, exceptions and promises are not success. Action kinds: revive, intimidate, unlock_safe, unlock_door, cut_fence, plant_bomb. Payload has memberId,targetId,live target/member,stats,actionId. `plant_bomb` callback occurs at detonation after completed planting + fuse, once. Before that use onAction to show attachment/countdown. It follows target identity even when the car moves. Dismissal and operator death do not delete an armed charge. Pre-plant invalid target/death/path timeout cancel. Existing server mechanics must be used by the adapter.

Additional lifecycle effects: hospitalize `{memberId,member,hospitalUntil}` and discharge `{memberId,member,stats}`. Hospitalize confirms source relocation/inactivation. Discharge must restore life at the hospital and launch normal walking/follow; never teleport to player. Failed lifecycle effects retry at most once per second. Confirmed death enters hospital immediately; downed members have 20 seconds for a medic before 300-second hospitalization. Hospital deadlines persist as epoch timestamps. Hospital dismissal prevents return. Weapon metadata and progression persist through hospitalization. Medic scans bounded player + squad candidates twice per second.

Methods: recruit({id,profession,name}), dismiss(id) returning removed member metadata, setWeapon(id,metadata), command(memberId,kind,targetId), cancel(id), update(), addXP(id,amount), upgrade(id,skill), stats(id), availableActions(targetOrId), getRoster(), getAction(id), snapshot(), restore(data). Root handles transactional gun inventory transfer; setWeapon stores only metadata. stats returns multipliers and reviveFraction, never applies HP. Save version1 holds roster/progression/weapon/hospital deadline, not HP or world positions. Restore rejects while commands are active. Armed charges serialize in optional version1 charges with stable actionId/memberId/targetId, armedAt/detonateAt epoch timestamps, phase and preserved sequence. Restored expired fuses fire once on the next update at live target position; missing targets cancel. Dismissed operators do not prevent charge restoration. Unarmed work is cancelled by reload. Up to 4096 settled action receipts persist to suppress replay; source effects should additionally be idempotent by actionId when importing older world saves.

Balance provisional: squad5, level per100XP (max20), success25XP, skill ranks0–5, HP bruiser1.65x/melee1.8x; fitness +8% HP/rank, melee+12%, specialist duration / (1+.12rank), medic35%+5%HP/rank capped80%. Work durations revive4/intimidate2.5/safe8/door5/fence6/bomb4 sec; fuse6, retreat8m, bomb cooldown20 sec. No ammunition stock is decremented for specialist C4. These are tunable defaults, not previously approved prices or final balance.
