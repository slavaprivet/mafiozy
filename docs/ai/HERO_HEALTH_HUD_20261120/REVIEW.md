# Hero vitals integration, 20 November 2026

The old `Здоровье 100` line belonged to the balloon host and showed only its fall-damage pool. The new session-local `hero_vitals.gd` drives the left HUD from combat, catapult, melee-contact, fire-contact and balloon fall damage. Sword's per-bone HP/sever state remains in place for existing combat mechanics; confirmed events synchronize the 15-segment HUD. An absent hand or foot is displayed at its exact segment, without falsely removing an entire arm or leg. Optional melee-owner and sword HP fields are compatibility mirrors.

A visible accepted skin cut adds a finite eight-second blood-loss source. A committed stump adds a twelve-second source. Up to four active sources are counted, matching BloodFlow32's per-host source budget. Their HP/s rates add, with 6 HP/s displayed as 100% bleeding. Damage is applied in 0.1-second ticks. Blocked or blunt hits add no bleeding. No per-frame scene traversal or new HUD nodes are created during combat.

The burning indicator is set by accepted Molotov damage ticks and actual player contact with castle fire. Castle fire checks at most six active flames once per 0.5 seconds with a distance test and a physics ray; it applies 2 HP per accepted tick. The indicator expires after fire contact ends. It never marks the player burning simply because an object elsewhere is on fire.

The current hero mesh does not support torso bisection: a fatal chest/pelvis blow kills but does not separate the torso. HUD `body_split` requires both a committed torso sever and real mesh-cut receipt; it therefore stays false under current combat instead of showing an invented split. The whole scene reloads on R/F5, reconstructing full HP, healthy parts and zero bleeding/burning.

`test_hero_vitals_integration.gd` checks multiple cuts, exact hand and head loss, truthful torso-split gating, wound-source capacity, bleed expiration, blunt damage, fire contact and wall occlusion, status expiry, bleedout and scene reload. It passed on Godot 4.7.2 on 20 November 2026 (`HERO_VITALS PASS`, exit 0). A separate shield regression also reported `SHIELD_RESULT PASS`; its fixture emitted an unrelated null-parameter diagnostic. DeepSeek was consulted through the protected client (`deepseek_response.txt` and `deepseek_final_response.txt`); it recommended a single owner and bounded event-driven wounds. Its suggestion to remove sword's existing sever mechanics was not adopted because that would change combat behavior beyond this HUD task. First `deepseek-flash` attempt produced an empty response; a repeat with `deepseek-chat` produced the saved review. No credentials are stored here.
